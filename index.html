Automotive Photography

Shooting cars is a craft of light, geometry and timing. Every shoot is planned around the car — its lines, its surfaces, the way it reads against a landscape or studio backdrop. The goal is a frame that feels intentional and built for the brand it represents.

Work has covered commercial campaigns, dealer and launch material, editorial features and enthusiast events across Estonia and the wider Baltic and Nordic region.

Feature Photo

Videography & Motion

Beyond stills, motion brings the car to life — sound, movement and pace working together. From short brand films and social cut-downs to launch reels and longer documentary pieces, the approach stays the same: clean composition, strong colour and a story built around the car.

Available for full production — directing, shooting, editing and grade — or as a DP on larger crews.
