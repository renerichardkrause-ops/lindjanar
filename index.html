Automotive Photography

There is a way every car wants to be photographed — a line that catches the light first, a stance that calls for a low angle, a paint that comes alive at dusk. The work is finding that combination and building the frame around it.

Commercial campaigns, dealer and launch material, editorial features and enthusiast shoots across Estonia, the Baltics and beyond.

Videography & Motion

Cars are made for motion. Where stills hold a moment, film shows weight, sound and pace — the way a car actually lives.

Available for full production — directing, shooting, editing and grade — or as a DP on larger crews. Short brand films, launch reels, social cut-downs and longer documentary pieces.

Retouching

Every frame on this site has been through retouching — it is part of the photography workflow, not a separate service. Raw files are graded for colour and contrast, distractions cleaned up, skies and surfaces balanced, and the car brought forward where it needs to be.

The aim stays the same: keep the photograph honest, but make sure nothing pulls the eye away from the car. Drag the handle below to compare an untouched frame against the finished image.

Photography Kit

The kit is built for two things — handling a full commercial shoot reliably, and travelling light when the work calls for it. Modern mirrorless bodies, fast primes and zooms, a portable flash kit for location, and the rigging needed to mount, light and stabilise on moving cars.

For motion work the same bodies are used with cinema lenses, follow-focus and external monitoring. For aerials, a drone capable of 4K and slow-motion fills in the gaps a tripod cannot reach.

Janar will list current bodies, lenses, lighting, rigging and support here in full — so producers can plan around what is on the truck.

A Passion For Cars

Photography started with the cars first. Long before any client work there were car meets, late drives and a camera that came along for the ride. That part has not changed — there is still an ongoing personal archive of project cars, road trips and the kind of scenes that never make it onto a brief.
